RK3576 — Firmware Flash

Flash edk2-rk3576 UEFI or mainline U-Boot: to SPI NOR on ROCK 4D, to eMMC on CM5-IO. Requires Chrome or Edge (WebUSB). No drivers needed on Linux / macOS.

  1. Select your board above
  2. Hold the MaskROM button, plug in USB-C, then release
  3. Click a flash button and pick the Rockchip device from the browser prompt
  4. After the loader is sent, click Reconnect Device and pick the device again
  5. Wait for Flash complete — the board reboots automatically
